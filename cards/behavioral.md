## bh-conflict | Tell me about a technical disagreement and how you resolved it.
- STAR: situation, task, action, result with a measurable outcome
- Shows listening, data or prototype over opinion, and what the team gained
- Owns own part; no blaming

## bh-failure | Tell me about a failure or production incident you caused or handled.
- Concrete incident, own role, timeline of detection and mitigation
- Root cause and the systemic fix (tests, alerts, process), not just the patch
- What you do differently now

## bh-impact | Describe your most impactful project.
- Problem and why it mattered, with numbers (latency, cost, users, time saved)
- Your specific contribution vs. the team's; key technical decision and trade-offs
- Result and what you would change
